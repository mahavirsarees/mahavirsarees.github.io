Wholesale Sarees in Rewari

In shortMahavir Trading Company, the firm behind Mahavir Sarees, is a saree and lehenga wholesaler at Mahavir Chowk, Bajaja Bazaar, Rewari. Shop owners, boutiques and bulk buyers get wholesale rates on every design. Call or WhatsApp 99207 53269 for rates.

Who we supply

Retail saree shops, boutiques and bulk buyers in Rewari and nearby towns buy stock from us. Mahavir Trading Company is our wholesale firm; Mahavir Sarees is our in-house brand. Single-piece customers are welcome too, at the same shop.

What you can buy wholesale

  • Silk sarees: Banarasi, Kanjivaram and Pashmina silk
  • Bridal sarees and designer sarees
  • Designer lehengas, lehenga chunni and girlish laccha
  • Our own designs: Rivaz, Silsila, Mastani, Dulhan

New designs arrive every week. Wholesale rates are shared on call or WhatsApp, not on the website.

How to order wholesale

  1. Call or WhatsApp 99207 53269 with your shop name and city.
  2. Tell us the designs or fabrics you want, or ask for photos of current stock.
  3. Visit the shop at Mahavir Chowk to see stock in person, or confirm the order on WhatsApp.
