About Mahavir Sarees, Rewari

In shortMahavir Sarees is a saree and lehenga house at Mahavir Chowk, Bajaja Bazaar, Rewari, Haryana, right in front of the Hanuman Mandir. We have served Rewari since 2011, with wholesale rates for retailers, boutiques and bulk buyers, and single pieces for every customer.

Who we are

Mahavir Sarees is the in-house brand of Mahavir Trading Company, a saree wholesaler and retailer in Rewari. For more than 15 years, families from Rewari and nearby towns have come to our shop in Bajaja Bazaar for wedding, festival and everyday sarees. Shop owners and boutiques come to us for wholesale stock. Mahavir Sarees is a family business: it belongs to Atul Sharma's father, and Atul Sharma runs it day to day, from buying and wholesale to customer orders and this website. Our tagline is Kare Rishton Ki Shuruwat, because a saree is so often part of a family's biggest days.

What we sell

  • Silk sarees, including pure Banarasi silk, Kanjivaram silk and Pashmina silk
  • Bridal sarees and designer sarees
  • Designer lehengas, lehenga chunni and dupattas
  • Suits and dress material
  • Girlish laccha

New designs arrive every week. Our newest sarees online are Rivaz, Silsila, Mastani and Dulhan.

Wholesale and retail

Retailers, boutiques and bulk buyers get wholesale rates on every design. If you are buying a single saree, you are just as welcome, at the shop or on WhatsApp 99207 53269.

Shipping and exchange

We ship across India for free. Cash on delivery is available with ₹250 paid in advance. Sarees can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery if the tag is attached. Read the full Shipping & Exchange Policy.

We write simple guides from the questions customers ask us at the counter: how to check real Banarasi silk, how to choose a colour for your skin tone, and how to care for silk and zari. See all guides on our blog.
