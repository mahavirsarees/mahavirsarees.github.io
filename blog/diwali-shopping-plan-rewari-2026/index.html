Diwali Shopping in Rewari: A Simple Plan for Your Festive Saree

8 November 2026·Mahavir Sarees, Rewari

संक्षेप मेंरेवाड़ी में दिवाली की साड़ी की खरीदारी को आसान बनाने के लिए पहले से तय करें कि साड़ी किस दिन और किस शाम के लिए है: धनतेरस (6 नवंबर), दिवाली (8 नवंबर) या मेल-मुलाकात। ब्लाउज़ के लिए कुछ दिन अलग रखें, और दुकान आने से पहले रंग और बजट की सूची बना लें। रविवार का समय फ़ोन पर 093548 27020 से पूछ लें।

Diwali shopping in Rewari tends to come all at once. Dhanteras is on Friday, 6 November, Diwali is on Sunday, 8 November, and the family visits start straight after. Leave the saree to the last evening and you will choose in a hurry, with the blouse still not stitched. A little planning makes the same week calmer. This is a simple plan for buying your festive saree, whether you come to our shop at Bajaja Bazaar or order from another town.

Start with the calendar, not the shop

Work backwards from the days you need the saree:

  • Dhanteras, Friday 6 November. Many families buy something new on this day. If you are choosing a gift, decide early so there is time to pick the right colour.
  • Diwali, Sunday 8 November. This is the puja evening. Choose a saree you can sit, bend and stand in for a few hours.
  • Visits and Diwali milan, the days after. These need a comfortable saree you can wear from house to house.

Then add the blouse to the plan. Stitching takes time, and a festival week fills up fast. Give the blouse the first slot, and leave yourself a few extra days.

Make a short list before you come

Ten minutes at home saves an hour in the shop. Write down:

  • Who the saree is for, and which evening it is for: puja, visits or a party.
  • Your budget. If you are ordering from outside Rewari, include the shipping. Every price on our website includes shipping.
  • The colours your family likes, and any colour your family avoids for the occasion.
  • A blouse piece, or a photo of a blouse that fits you well.
  • Your blouse measurements, if you want the blouse stitched after you buy.

If you are buying gifts as well, our Dhanteras and Diwali gift guide covers how to choose for a mother, a sister, a new bahu and staff.

What to check in the shop

Once you are in front of the sarees, take your time. Hold the fabric and feel its weight. Look at the border and the pallu closely, and check the work on both sides of the saree. Then look at the colour in daylight. Warm shop lights and diya light can make a colour look different, so a short walk to the door is worth it.

Ask us how the saree should be cleaned before you buy it. Some sarees need a dry cleaner, and it is better to know that at the start.

Pick a colour that works in photos too

Diwali week is full of photos: the puja, the family group, the visits. Choose a colour that looks good in warm lamp light and in daylight. Deep reds, maroons and yellows are the classic festive choices, and they hold up well on camera. Very pale shades can look washed out under flash, so hold the saree against your skin in daylight before you decide. If you are unsure, bring someone whose eye you trust.

After Diwali: the wedding season

If there is a wedding in your family, the muhurats from around 21 November are when the next rush starts. Our wedding season guide explains how to choose a saree for each function. Start early, and confirm the muhurat dates with your pandit before you plan the shopping.

Keep the festive saree looking good

After the festival, store your saree the right way. Air it fully before you fold it, wrap it in cotton or muslin, and keep it out of damp cupboards. Our silk and zari care guide has the simple steps for washing, ironing and storage.

Your Diwali shopping checklist

  1. Pick the day and the evening the saree is for.
  2. Choose the colour and fabric with the family.
  3. Give the blouse a few extra days.
  4. Visit in daylight, or send a photo on WhatsApp first.
  5. Check the Sunday timings by phone before you come.
