FAQ

Questions customers ask us

What are the shop timings? / Dukaan kab khulti hai?

Mahavir Sarees is open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM – 8:00 PM, and Sunday, 10:30 AM – 6:00 PM. Somvaar se Shanivaar subah 9:30 se raat 8 baje tak, aur Ravivaar 10:30 se shaam 6 baje tak.

Where is Mahavir Sarees in Rewari? / Dukaan kahan hai?

Mahavir Chowk, Bajaja Bazaar, Rewari, Haryana 123401, right in front of the Hanuman Mandir. Landmark: Mahavir Chowk ka Hanuman Mandir, dukaan uske theek saamne hai. Call 099207 53269 if you need directions.

Do you sell wholesale to shop owners and retailers?

Yes. We are a saree wholesaler and retailer in Rewari. Shop owners, boutiques and bulk buyers get wholesale rates. Wholesale enquiries ke liye call ya WhatsApp karein: 99207 53269.

Can I order on WhatsApp? / WhatsApp pe order ho sakta hai?

Haan. Saree ka photo ya screenshot WhatsApp 99207 53269 pe bhejiye, hum rate, colours aur availability bata denge. Rates vary by design and fabric, so please ask on WhatsApp for the current price.

Do you ship sarees across India? Is COD available?

Yes, we ship across India and shipping is free. Prices on our product pages include shipping. COD (cash on delivery) is available with ₹250 paid in advance. Order details ke liye WhatsApp 99207 53269 karein.

Can I return or exchange a saree? / Saree exchange ho sakti hai?

Exchange only, no refunds. You can exchange a saree within 7 days of delivery if its tag is still attached. If the tag is broken, removed or missing, the saree cannot be exchanged. You pay the courier charge to send it back. Read the full Shipping & Exchange Policy.

What kinds of sarees do you have?

Pure Banarasi silk, Kanjivaram silk and Pashmina silk sarees, bridal sarees, designer sarees, designer lehengas and girlish laccha, plus new arrivals like our Silk Jerken and Weightless Silk sarees. Shaadi, festival aur daily wear, sab ke liye collection hai.

Can I buy a single saree, or only in bulk?

Single pieces are welcome. Ek saree lena ho toh bhi aaiye; every customer can buy single pieces at the shop or on WhatsApp, and bulk buyers get wholesale rates.

What are your saree prices? / Saree ka rate kya hai?

Rates depend on the design and fabric. Each saree's own page on this website shows its current price, shipping included. Kisi aur design ka rate jaanne ke liye uska photo ya screenshot WhatsApp 99207 53269 pe bhejiye. Retailers and boutiques can ask for wholesale rates on the same number.

Do your prices include shipping? / Kya shipping charge alag lagega?

All prices shown on our product pages include shipping. Shipping sabhi rates mein shamil hai. For COD orders and order details, WhatsApp 99207 53269.

What is the phone number of Mahavir Sarees, Rewari?

Call the shop on 099207 53269. For WhatsApp orders, rates and wholesale enquiries, message 99207 53269. Dukaan Mahavir Chowk, Bajaja Bazaar, Hanuman Mandir ke saamne hai.

Do you have bridal sarees for the wedding season? / Shaadi ke liye bridal saree milegi?

Yes. We have bridal sarees, designer sarees and designer lehengas, including our Dulhan HO Pure Silk Saree in Red, Rani, Yellow and Green. Visit the shop with your family to see the full collection, or WhatsApp 99207 53269 with the look you want.

Can I see a saree before I buy? / Saree dekh kar hi lena hai?

Yes. Visit the shop and see the saree in daylight. If you are not in Rewari, send a photo of the design on WhatsApp 99207 53269 and we will reply with the rate and colours.

What should I bring to match a blouse? / Blouse match karne ke liye kya laayein?

Bring the blouse, or a piece of its fabric, so the colour can be checked against the saree in daylight. For details, WhatsApp 99207 53269.
