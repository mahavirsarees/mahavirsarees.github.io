Shipping & Exchange Policy

Shipping

  • Free shipping across India on every order. The price you see already includes shipping.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is available. A small advance payment is needed for COD orders. WhatsApp 99207 53269 for the details.

Poore India mein free shipping. Jo rate dikh raha hai usme shipping shamil hai. COD available hai, bas thoda advance dena hota hai. Details ke liye WhatsApp 99207 53269.

Exchange policy

  • Exchange only, no refunds. You can exchange a saree within 7 days of delivery.
  • The tag must be attached. If the tag is broken, removed or missing, the saree cannot be exchanged.
  • You pay the courier charge to send the saree back to us.
  • To start an exchange, WhatsApp 99207 53269 with your order details and photos of the saree and its tag.

Delivery ke 7 din ke andar exchange ho sakta hai, refund nahi hota. Tag laga hona zaroori hai. Tag toota, nikla ya gum hua toh exchange nahi hoga. Wapas bhejne ka courier kharcha customer ka hoga.
