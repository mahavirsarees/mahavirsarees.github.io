Before you visit

  • Call 099207 53269 to check the timing, especially on a Sunday.
  • Bring a blouse piece if you have one, so the colour match is easier to judge.
  • Tell us the occasion and your budget, and we will show you what suits it.

Finding the shop

Ask for Hanuman Mandir at Bajaja Bazaar. Our shop is in front of the temple, at Mahavir Chowk. If you are coming by car, the Google Maps link above will give you directions from where you are.

Ordering from outside Rewari

Shipping is free across India and included in all prices. Cash on delivery is available with ₹250 paid in advance. Send us the design you like on WhatsApp, and we will tell you the price and colours.

Wholesale and single pieces

We sell single pieces for family occasions, and we also work with buyers who need more than one saree. For wholesale, message us on WhatsApp at 99207 53269.
